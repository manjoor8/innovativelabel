Production Asset

Flexographic Printing

Flexography is the backbone of our manufacturing operation. Utilizing high-efficiency rotary plates and UV drying systems, we produce high-definition labels and packaging at high speeds, guaranteeing color consistency across large B2B production runs.

Production Machinery Fleet Specs

Taiyo STF 340

10 Colors | Width: 340 mm

Our flagship Japanese-engineered flexo press. Ideal for complex multi-web labels, booklet labels, and high-color decorations.

  • 10 UV print stations
  • Dual die-cutting slots
  • Booklet label insertion module
  • Delam/Relam print features

Alliance A3

8 Colors | Width: 330 mm

High-speed web press optimized for filmic label runs and shrink sleeves, delivering high register accuracy.

  • 8 UV print stations
  • Chill drums for temperature-sensitive thin films
  • Electronic register controls

Mark Andy 2200

8 Colors | Width: 250 mm

Reliable workhorse press designed for short to medium runs of self-adhesive labels and industrial tags.

  • 8 UV and hot-air print stations
  • Inline sheeting conveyor
  • Double die-cut rotary module

Core Strengths

Up to 10 colors in a single pass
Inline cold foiling, laminating, and die-cutting
UV-cured inks for instant drying and chemical resistance
Precise registration control for multi-layer labeling
