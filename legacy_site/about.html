Company Overview

The company is managed by a team of professionals with more than 20 years of experience in the printing industry.

Innovative Label Solutions Pvt Ltd expertise's in manufacturing of Shrink Sleeves, Cartons, and Pressure Sensitive / Self Adhesive Labels in roll form, suitable for application by advance automatic / semi automatic labeling machines, as well as in sheet form for manual application.

Our plant, spread over 25,000 sqf area, is located in the fastest emerging Industrial Zone of Mumbai suburb, Dombivali MIDC, which is 15 minutes walking distance from Dombivali Station.
